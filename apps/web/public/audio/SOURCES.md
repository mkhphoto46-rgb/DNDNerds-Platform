# Town ambience sources

- `village-market.ogg`: [Market recorded audio.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Market_recorded_audio.ogg), Aishatu98, CC0 1.0. Original recording, unmodified.
- `inn-crowd.ogg`: [Restaurant ambience.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Restaurant_ambience.ogg), stephan, public domain. Original recording, unmodified. Used as the inn's crowd ambience.
- `fountain-water.mp3`: [Water Fountain Sound](https://freesound.org/people/Legnalegna55/sounds/554789/) by Legnalegna55, CC0. Freesound's high-quality preview is used as a looping fountain effect.
- `sheep-bleat.ogg`: [Sheep bleat.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sheep_bleat.ogg) by Eviatar Bach, CC0 1.0. Plays once when a visitor clicks a sheep.
- `sheep-bleat-loud.mp3`: the same CC0 sheep recording, amplified by 21 dB and encoded as MP3 for louder mobile playback; peak remains about 6 dB below clipping.

All ambience plays only after the visitor enables town audio. Water and tavern crowd volume follow the camera's distance from those places; playback stops when audio is disabled or the scene unmounts.
