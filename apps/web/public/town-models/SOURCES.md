# Town 3D assets

Selected GLB models and `Textures/colormap.png` come from [Kenney's Fantasy Town Kit 2.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit), licensed CC0 1.0. The original `LICENSE.txt` is included here. The buildings, road, fountain, people, camera path, and polyhedral dice in the homepage are constructed in project code.

The original kit archive is preserved at `assets/source/kenney_fantasy-town-kit_2.0.zip` for traceability.
